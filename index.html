    Cobb County DOT262 stops

    County maintained right of way, including Mableton, which buys its transportation services from Cobb DOT under an intergovernmental agreement. 152 of these stops already have a sidewalk by the OpenStreetMap read. One conversation covers a large share of the network.

    Georgia DOT235 stops

    State routes. Cobb Pkwy (US 41 / SR 3), Austell Rd (SR 5), South Cobb Dr (SR 280), Powder Springs St (SR 360), Marietta Pkwy (SR 120), Mableton Pkwy (SR 139). Highest service, hardest permit. Cobb sits in GDOT District 7.

    • Permitting and GPAS portal
    • Driveway and Encroachment Control, Ch. 7 (PDF)
    • Area office contacts (PDF)
    Cities114 stops

    Stops inside Marietta, Smyrna and Austell on roads that are not state routes. Cobb does not publish who maintains each road, so "city" here means inside city limits and not a state route. Some of these are county arterials. Each city sets its own ROW rules; confirm before asking.

    • Marietta, 74 stops
    • Smyrna, 40 stops
    • mariettaga.gov · smyrnaga.gov · austellga.gov
    Out of scope14 stops

    8 stops sit in Fulton County, the MARTA Arts Center and H.E. Holmes tails of routes 10, R10, 25 and 30. Another 6 are on private property or mall ring roads that could not be resolved. Different jurisdiction, different ask.

    Where the data came from
    • Service levels, stop names, coordinates CobbLinc GTFS, feed version Oct 19 2026, valid to Mar 27 2027. gtfs-zip.ashx
    • Road names and class Cobb County GIS, Roads (open data). No maintainer or speed limit field, so speed is unknown for every stop. MapServer layer 0
    • State routes GDOT State Route Network, mainline routes within 90 ft of a stop. MapServer layer 0
    • Sidewalk presence OpenStreetMap: separate sidewalk ways within 15 ft of the stop, or a sidewalk tag on the road for the side the stop is on. Cobb sells its sidewalk layer rather than publishing it. Overpass API
    • City limits and county boundary Cobb Boundaries MapServer
    • Everything else Cobb County Open Data · CobbLinc

    Read this before you trust it. Sidewalk means OpenStreetMap has a sidewalk mapped near the stop. OSM coverage in Cobb is incomplete, so "no sidewalk" can mean "nobody mapped it yet". It says nothing about width, condition, or whether there is room for a bench without blocking the pedestrian route. GTFS coordinates can sit 30 to 60 ft off the actual pole. Trips per weekday is service, not ridership: it tells you how long you wait, not how many people wait. Every stop needs eyes on it before anything gets built.
